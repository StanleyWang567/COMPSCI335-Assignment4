About

New Zealand Sign Language (NZSL), or Te Reo Turi, is one of Aotearoa New Zealand's three official languages. NZSL is a rich and expressive language used by Deaf people and the wider Deaf community throughout New Zealand.

This application has been designed to help beginners take their first steps in learning NZSL. Explore a selection of signs, practise your vocabulary, discover upcoming events, and connect with other learners through the guest book.
Your NZSL journey starts here.
